About Us

Our Company

Our company is situated at a prime location in Delhi, India. We are acknowledged and prestigious pencils manufacturer. We deal in a diverse variety of pencils that are used for writing and sketching purposes. These pencils are made from quality assured raw materials that are non toxic and environment friendly. Therefore our products does not harm the kids in any manner. We manufacture pencils in large quantities every month so that we can meet with the demands of the market. Please feel free to contact us for more imformation.

Our company is one of the reputed names in the industry engaged in offering Paper Pencils. Our offered pencils are made by our skilled professionals in a solid pigment core that is placed inside a protective casing of wood, plastic or various other substances. To meet varied demands of the customers, we offer these pencils with different shapes, designs, colors and magnificent patterns.

Our offered Multi Color Velvet Polymer Pencil is made utilizing optimum grade raw materials. The raw material source from trusted ad reliable suppliers of the market. We provide this range of product at market leading rates.
