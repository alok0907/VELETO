We are a leading manufacturer of Eco-Friendly Velvet pencils. We also manufacture Paper Pencils and Wood Pencils. We deal with clients from all over India.

Velvet Pencils

Velvet pencils are made from 100% recycled paper, and available in multiple colors that are attractive and soft to hold. Velvet paper pencils are available in vibrant colours offering great user experience.

Paper pencils

Our paper pencil is far better than other "recycled" pencils. Generally the "recycled" pencils are made from recycled fibers which have been shredded, mixed with a hard resin, then extruded around a hard lead core.

Wooden Pencils

We all love using wooden pencils for drawing, sketching, and everyday writing. Artists, students, writers, and designers alike have long relied on the versatility of wooden pencils for their work.
