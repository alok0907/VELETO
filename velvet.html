Velvet Pencils

Velvet pencils are made from 100% recycled paper, and available in multiple colors that are attractive and soft to hold.velvet paper pencils are available in vibrant colours offering great user experience. The pencils are made completely with sustainable materials with no plastic, no wood and zero toxic substances. These exquisite pencils aren't made from wood, instead they are made from recycled newspaper. With no wood and zero plastic, these pencils are both suitable for professionals as well as kids and are fun to use.
